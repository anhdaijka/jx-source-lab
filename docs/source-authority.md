# Source Authority & Evidence Policy

## Canon target
The lab targets the **latest coherent Kiếm Thế lore supported by the combined evidence corpus**. It is not trying to reconstruct one pristine launch-era client/server build.

## Practical authority
For story/lore claims, prefer the interpretation best supported across:
1. story-bearing raw client/server data;
2. Kingsoft/Xoyo first-party lore/story material;
3. VNG official localization/publication;
4. archived official material.

Agreement across independent source families raises confidence. Community/fan material remains lead-only unless independently corroborated.

## Modified-build policy
The supplied client/server may contain community modifications or added features. That fact alone does not reduce their narrative value when story-bearing tasks, dialogue, characters, factions, skills, items and locations remain consistent with the wider lore corpus.

Do not investigate exact build provenance merely to prove authenticity. Build/version/origin is supporting metadata, not a promotion requirement, unless a concrete discrepancy changes lore interpretation.

## Drift
Keep `EDITION_DRIFT` where detected, but distinguish its narrative impact:
- `NONE` — implementation/config difference with no story consequence;
- `POSSIBLE` — may affect interpretation and deserves targeted checking;
- `MATERIAL` — changes a load-bearing story/lore claim and must be reconciled or explicitly held unresolved.

Only `MATERIAL` narrative drift blocks the affected claim/arc from promotion.

## Confidence
`VERIFIED_DIRECT`, `VERIFIED_CROSS_SOURCE`, `STRONG`, `INFERENCE`, `UNKNOWN`, `CONFLICT`, `EDITION_DRIFT`.
The first three are normally promotable. `STRONG` must state its basis. `INFERENCE` may support navigation/research but cannot silently become canon.

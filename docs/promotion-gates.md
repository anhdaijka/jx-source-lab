# Promotion Gates

- **S0 Inventory** — source roots and relevant file families catalogued. Exact build/edition identity is optional unless it changes narrative interpretation.
- **S1 Parser reliability** — representative samples parse correctly with provenance.
- **S2 Domain corpus** — separate PASS for quests/dialogue, NPC, sect/skills, items, locations/features, assets.
- **S3 Story reconstruction** — evidence-backed causal dossiers; central UNKNOWNs explicit; non-central gaps may remain.
- **S4 Cross-source lore validation** — load-bearing story/lore claims checked across raw story-bearing data and Kingsoft/Xoyo/VNG/archived official material where available. Only narrative-significant drift requires arbitration.
- **S5 Research Release** — manifests, corpus snapshots, unresolved central questions, confidence report and promotable game-story dossiers complete.

Promotion is based on **story/lore sufficiency**, not provenance perfection or exact version archaeology. Non-narrative implementation drift, custom feature differences and non-central UNKNOWNs do not block promotion.

Only S5 material seeds the future novel repo. Novel continuity never overrides a later better-supported source correction.
